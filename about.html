My Hobbies & Interests

Playing Basketball

I enjoy playing basketball in open gyms and in the winter Men's leagues in my hometown. I also enjoy watching basketball and following the NBA. My favorite team is the Los Angeles Lakers and my favorite basketball player of all time is Lebron James.

Drones

I enjoy capturing aerial footage of cool landscapes and filming videos for my former high school during events such as football games, marching band shows, and parades.

Football

I enjoy watching football and following the NFL. My favorite team is the Baltimore Ravens!

My Dogs

I love spending time with my dogs. I have 5 dogs: One Siberian Husky (Sonic) and four German Shepherds (Nala, Lucy, Thanos, & Peluchin). The ones in the image are: Peluchin, Lucy, and Thanos (from left to right). Sonic and Nala are the parents of the three German Shepherds in the picture. Nala had a litter of 9 puppies back on February 19, 2025 and I ended up keeping a great trio.

Gaming

I enjoy gaming in my free time and competing with friends. I mainly game on PC, PS5, Xbox Series X, Nintendo Switch, & Mobile. My favorite games to play are Madden, College Football, GTA Online, CS2, Fortnite, Valorant, Overwatch, Super Mario Maker 2, & Clash of Clans.

Drumming

I enjoy playing the drums and following along to my favorite songs. I started playing the drums at the age of 4-years old for my church back in El Salvador.

Video Editing

I enjoy editing videos on Davinci Resolve that were taken from my drone or 360 action camera and putting together cenematic videos to display the memories that were captured.
